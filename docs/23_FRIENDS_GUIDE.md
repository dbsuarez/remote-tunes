# RemoteTunes: a short guide

A music player for the songs on your phone, with a few extras. This is the quick version. Everything is also in the app under **Menu > Help**.

---

## 1. The basics

- **Bottom bar:** *Now Playing*, *Library*, *Search* and *Queue*. A small player sits under every tab except Now Playing; tap it to open the big one.
- **Menu button (top left):** the quickest way to anything: Lyrics, Playlists, Equalizer, Insights, Settings, Help.
- **Now Playing:** the cover, a seek bar, and a row of buttons for favorite, shuffle, repeat, speed and sleep timer. Swipe in from the **left edge** for your Library and from the **right edge** to raise the queue.

---

## 2. Getting your music in

Tap **Library**, then the **+** button.

- **Add folder:** the best way. It reads your music folder where it is (nothing is copied). You can untick folders you do not want, and it remembers your choice.
- **Add songs:** picks single files and keeps a copy inside the app.
- **Rescan music folders:** after you put new songs in your folder, one tap adds them (and follows songs you moved).

**Leave things out by name:** Settings > Library > *Excluded from library* > *Add a rule*, for example *intro* to skip every file with "intro" in its name.

---

## 3. Playing

- Tap a song to play it; the rest of the list joins the queue. **Long-press** to pick several songs, then add them to the queue or a playlist.
- A song's **menu (three dots)** has Play next, Add to queue, Add to playlist and **Edit info**.
- **Playlists:** make one from the Library. Add songs from almost any list.
- **Shuffle** plays every song once before it repeats any.
- **Lyrics:** on Now Playing, tap the lyrics button on the cover. Timed lyrics follow the song; tap a line to jump to it.
- **Sleep timer** and **speed** are in the row of buttons on Now Playing.
- **Queue:** swipe a row left to remove it (with Undo), swipe right to add it to a playlist.

### Make the Library look the way you like
The sliders button at the top of a song list opens **List options**: titles only, a compact list, or a grid of covers. **Pinch** a list to make it bigger or smaller.

---

## 4. Sound

- **Equalizer** (Menu): presets, ten bands, bass and treble, and a **Compressor** that evens out loud and quiet parts (nice for talk or quiet listening). It can remember a setting for the speaker, wired headphones and Bluetooth separately.
- **Crossfade** (Settings > Playback and sound): songs fade into each other.
- **Even out loudness** (Replay Gain): songs that were recorded loud or quiet play at a similar level.
- **Visualizer:** moving bars with the music. Settings > Visualizer. You can put it on the cover and choose top, middle or bottom.

---

## 5. Audiobooks

Keep audiobooks in their own folder(s) and add each with **Library > + > Add audiobook folder**. They stay out of your music and shuffle, have their own **Audiobooks** category, and **remember where you stopped** (and the speed you like for each book).

---

## 6. Tidying your tags

**Settings > Library > Tag cleanup** finds songs with a missing artist, album, year or genre and fills them in, from the file names or from MusicBrainz (a free music database). You see every change first and tick what you want. A good tag is never replaced, and the last changes can be undone.

You can also fix a single song: its menu > **Edit info** (and *Look up this song* to find the details, lyrics and cover in one go), or pick several songs and edit what they share.

---

## 7. Your numbers

**Menu > Insights** shows how big your library is, your genres and top artists, what you play most, and how much you have listened this week. It is all worked out on your phone.

---

## 8. Keeping your things safe

**Settings > Backup and restore** saves your playlists, favorites, play counts, edits, lyrics, covers and settings to one file (no music). Copy it to another phone, add your music there, and restore.

---

## 9. Two phones

A second phone with RemoteTunes can be **controlled from the first**: you pick the songs on one phone and the other plays. See **Menu > Remote playback** and the chip at the top of the app.

---

## 10. Privacy in one paragraph

The app keeps everything on your phone. It only goes online when **you** ask it to: looking up lyrics, finding album art, searching MusicBrainz in Tag cleanup, the Online Library, or checking for a new version. The full list, with how to switch each off, is on the privacy page in the app.

---

If something looks wrong, tell whoever gave you the app what you tapped and what you saw. **Settings > Diagnostics > Copy** gives them the details.
